# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Venturine is a project for founders and builders, and for the people who contribute to it. We want participation to be respectful, harassment-free and useful for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity or opinion.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion focused on the work: the code, the skills, the evidence and the docs.
- Be honest about uncertainty. Do not present guesses as facts, and do not fabricate sources, statistics or testimonials.
- Respect the privacy of others. Do not post other people's personal or confidential information, and do not paste real customer, financial or business-confidential data into issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, or deliberately misleading contributions, including fabricated evidence.
- Other conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any other place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict or ban people who violate this code.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer, Maddy Zohadi, through GitHub (account `maddyzohadi`). While the repository is private, reports go through that route. A dedicated private contact will be listed here before the repository becomes public. Reports will be handled as privately as practical.
